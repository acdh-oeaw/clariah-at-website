## CLARIAH-AT Roadshow 2026: Funded Projects in the Spotlight

This year, the [Austrian Centre for Digital Humanities (ACDH) of the Austrian Academy of Sciences](/consortium/austrian-academy-of-sciences/) is hosting the **CLARIAH-AT Roadshow**.

The event will take place on **9 November 2026, from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.**, at Bäckerstraße 13, 1010 Vienna.

Projects funded under the [2024 and 2025 calls](https://clariah.at/en/project-funding/) will present their results: During a lightning talk session, the **small-scale projects** will showcase their ideas, methods, and findings in a concise format, while their accompanying posters will be available online. The **large-scale projects** will provide more in-depth insights into their research activities, developments, and achievements in a separate programme session.

Registration for the event will open soon. Until then: **Save the Date!**

Immediately following the CLARIAH-AT Roadshow, the Austrian National Library (ÖNB) invites participants to attend the ÖNB Labs Symposium and continue the exchange of ideas. To facilitate attendance at both events, the Roadshow programme has been coordinated accordingly: the start of the ÖNB Labs Symposium has been scheduled so that interested participants can first attend the CLARIAH-AT Roadshow and subsequently take part in the ÖNB Labs Symposium.
