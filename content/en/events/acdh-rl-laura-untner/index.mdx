The Austrian Centre for Digital Humanities (ACDH of the Austrian Academy of Sciences) is excited about an engaging Research Lunch:

We welcome **Laura Untner**, who will give a talk on "Sappho Digital":

The project "**Sappho Digital**" explores how computer-assisted approaches can meaningfully enhance (inter-)textual analyses, with a particular focus on the potential of **semantic technologies for reception-oriented research**.

As a case study, the project models approximately 100 testimonies of the productive literary reception of the ancient Greek poet Sappho in the German-speaking world as Linked Data. It traces the interconnections between these works as well as their links to the surviving fragments of Sappho.

**Laura Untner** is a digital literary scholar and literature mediator. She is currently a research associate at the Excellence Cluster "Temporal Communities. Doing Literature in a Global Perspective" (Freie Universität Berlin). From 2020 to 2024, she was a staff member at the ACDH.
